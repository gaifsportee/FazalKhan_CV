Daily performance by phase
Impression growth is not business growth.

Broad match plus "maximize clicks" bought progressively cheaper, less relevant traffic. One generic search term quietly ate 13.5% of the budget at more than double the average cost. On 11 September the campaign was corrected: 76 negative keywords, hours matched to opening time, high-intent keywords, and a brand campaign.

The honest headline: 467 tracked customer actions (calls, direction requests, and menu views) at a low cost each. Not orders, not revenue. The measured numbers are strong without embellishment.
